Browser ROM Player

Drop this folder on Netlify, then either place a ROM in /roms/ and launch it by path, or choose a ROM from your computer. For Neo Geo arcade games, use arcade; EmulatorJS maps that to the FBNeo core.

Keep ROM and BIOS files that you are legally entitled to use. This page does not download ROMs.

Put your ROM at the path above. For Metal Slug X, keep it zipped, usually named mslugx.zip. If your dump/core requires Neo Geo BIOS, place neogeo.zip in /bios/.

File mode keeps the ROM local to your browser. It is useful if you do not want to deploy a ROM file to Netlify at all.
